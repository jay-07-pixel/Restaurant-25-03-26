Our Heritage

A Legacy of Flavours

Born in the heart of Old Delhi, Kailash carries forward four generations of culinary mastery. Our recipes are whispered secrets from royal Mughal kitchens — each spice hand-ground, each dish a masterpiece of patience and passion.

From the smoky tandoor ovens to the slow-simmered curries, every plate at Kailash is a love letter to India's incredible culinary diversity.
